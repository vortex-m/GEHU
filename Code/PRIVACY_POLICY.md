# Privacy Policy for LearningLoop Educational App

**Last Updated:** December 20, 2025

## Introduction

LearningLoop ("the App") is designed to provide offline-first educational assistance to students in Classes 9-12. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Email Address:** Collected during account registration for authentication purposes
- **User Profile Data:** Name, selected avatar, class/grade information
- **Quiz Results:** Scores and progress tracking data

### Usage Data
- **Crash Reports:** Anonymous crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability
- **Performance Metrics:** App performance data via Firebase Performance Monitoring
- **Analytics:** Anonymous usage statistics via Firebase Analytics

### Data Stored Locally
- **AI Model:** 717MB Gemma-3 language model stored on your device
- **Educational Content:** JSON curriculum data cached locally
- **RAG Cache:** Search index for faster AI responses

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Authentication:** To secure your account and enable login
2. **Progress Tracking:** To save your quiz scores and learning progress
3. **App Improvement:** To identify and fix bugs through crash reports
4. **Performance Optimization:** To monitor and improve app performance

## Data Storage and Security

### Firebase Services
- User authentication data is stored in **Firebase Authentication**
- Profile and progress data is stored in Local Storage and in **Firebase Realtime Database** and **Firestore**
- Data is protected by Firebase security rules limiting access to authenticated users only

### Local Storage
- AI model and curriculum data are stored locally on your device
- No sensitive personal information is stored unencrypted

### Security Measures
- ProGuard code obfuscation for release builds
- Backup disabled to prevent unauthorized data extraction
- Secure HTTPS connections for all Firebase communications

## Data Sharing

**We do NOT:**
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for marketing purposes

**We MAY share:**
- Anonymous crash and performance data with Firebase (Google)
- Aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage statistics

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View your stored data at any time through the app
- **Delete:** Request account deletion by contacting us
- **Modify:** Update your profile information within the app
- **Opt-out:** Disable analytics (though crash reports help us improve the app)

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:
- **Firebase Authentication** - User login
- **Firebase Realtime Database** - Data storage
- **Firebase Firestore** - Data storage
- **Firebase Crashlytics** - Crash reporting
- **Firebase Analytics** - Usage analytics
- **Firebase Performance** - Performance monitoring

Please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information on how Firebase handles data.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Offline Functionality

The App is designed to work offline after initial setup. The AI model runs entirely on your device without sending queries to external servers, ensuring your questions remain private.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted within the app and on our website. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained until account deletion
- **Quiz History:** Retained for progress tracking; can be cleared manually
- **Crash Logs:** Automatically deleted after 90 days
